Chorda privacy policy

Puzzles Maniacs. Last updated 19 November 2026.

Chorda is a puzzle game for iPhone. This page says what the game does with information about you. The short version: we do not collect anything ourselves, and the game shows advertising supplied by Google, which collects some information to do that.

What we collect

Nothing. Chorda has no accounts and we run no analytics of our own. We do not receive your name, email address, contacts or anything about how you play.

What stays on your device

Your progress (levels solved, stars, badges, hints, spare lives, gold coins, looks you have bought, streaks, daily gifts, ad-free time) and your settings (theme, sound) are saved on your iPhone so the game can pick up where you left off. This information is not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising

Chorda shows ads from Google AdMob: a short ad between some levels, and videos you choose to watch in exchange for a hint, a life, a little more time or some gold coins. To deliver and measure these ads, Google's advertising software inside the app collects some information from your device, such as its IP address (which indicates your general location), a device identifier, which ads you have seen and how you interacted with them, and diagnostic and performance data. Google and its advertising partners use this for advertising and analytics. We do not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, and we do not receive this information ourselves.

How Google uses this information is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before showing ads, and you can change your answer at any time under Settings, "Ad privacy choices", in the game.

Buying ad-free time in the Store removes the ads between levels for as long as it lasts.

Purchases

Optional packs of lives, hints and gold coins, and ad-free time, are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details. Apple may give us totals of sales that do not identify you. Apple's own privacy policy covers what Apple does with your information.

Children

Chorda is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If the game changes in a way that affects this policy, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions: support@puzzlesmaniacs.com
