How to play

Drag from one numbered button to the next to thread them together. Each thread adds up its numbers and puts the total in the box of its colour. Use every number, never cross two threads, and make the equation true. Tap a box to choose which colour you are threading; tap a threaded number to snip it loose.

The tutorial can be replayed at any time: Settings, then How to play.

Common questions

I keep running out of time. Every level has a clock: 30 seconds on Easy, a minute on Medium, a minute and a half on Hard. The hourglass beside the clock adds 30 seconds for 200 gold coins. When the clock runs out you can watch a short video or spend 200 coins for 15 more seconds; otherwise it costs a life and the clock starts again.

How do I get gold coins? Finish a level with time to spare: every second left pays 2 coins, the first time you solve that level. Open the daily gift box on the home screen, watch a video in the Store, or buy a pack of coins there. Coins buy lives, hints, extra time and new looks.

There is no sound. Chorda follows the ring/silent switch: with the iPhone on silent, the game is silent too. Sound and music can also be switched off in Settings inside the game.

I bought something and did not get it. Close the game fully and open it again; purchases that were interrupted are delivered at the next launch. For ad-free time, the Store in the game has a Restore purchases link. If it is still missing, write to us with the date of the purchase.

Can I move my progress to a new phone? Progress is kept on the device and comes across when you restore the new phone from a backup of the old one.

I want a refund. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
