Thread the numbers. Make the equation true.

Chorda is a number puzzle for iPhone. Every board is an equation with coloured boxes. Drag a thread through the numbers, each colour adds up into its box, and no two threads may cross.

400 puzzles and six quests

From plain sums to brackets, little pictures and two equations at once. Solve a board without a slip and it earns a Perfect star.

A calculator made of cogs

Not sure what each colour should add up to? Turn the cogs and try the numbers before you commit a thread.

Something new every day

Five fresh daily puzzles, the same for everyone, and a gift box of gold coins to open each day you play.
